# Photos & Assets Needed from ISOC Jamaica

This list covers every image the website uses and what should replace it. Until the real files arrive, the site uses:

- **People:** a neutral grey "photo coming soon" silhouette (`public/assets/images/person-placeholder.svg`).
- **Everything else:** development placeholder photos borrowed from the global Internet Society website (`public/assets/images/photos/`). These are **not** ISOC Jamaica's own photos. Replace them, or confirm permission to use them, before launch.

Most photos can be uploaded by staff in the **admin dashboard** without a developer (column "Where to change it"). Images marked "developer" are part of the page design and need a file swap.

**Photo guidance:** use real Jamaican people, events and places, not generic stock photos. Use JPG or WebP, under about 500 KB each, and at least the sizes below. Landscape means wider than tall; portrait means taller than wide.
